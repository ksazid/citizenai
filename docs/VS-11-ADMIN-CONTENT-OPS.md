# VS-11 — Admin / Content Ops

## Scope

- Source refresh/change detection through content hashes.
- Changed fact → affected question impact analysis.
- Human approve/reject gates for facts and questions.
- Fact publication remains blocked unless source evidence and verification rules pass.
- Question approval remains blocked unless Question → Concept → Fact → Evidence provenance is valid.
- Pack publication requires both content approval and release approval.
- Published packs are immutable snapshots.

## Governance

AI may propose content changes but cannot independently establish canonical facts or publish a pack. Human review remains authoritative.
