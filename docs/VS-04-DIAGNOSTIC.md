# VS-04 — Adaptive Diagnostic

The diagnostic establishes an uncontaminated baseline before explanations are shown.

## Rules
- Target 20–30 questions.
- Prioritize important, difficult, and uncovered concepts.
- Do not finish before the minimum question count.
- Early completion additionally requires full domain coverage and at least 80% important-concept coverage.
- Domain summaries are weighted by concept importance rather than raw question percentage.
- Diagnostic data seeds mastery; it is not itself the final readiness score.
