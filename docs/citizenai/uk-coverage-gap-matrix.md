## Activation blockers

The pack must **not** be presented as exam-complete until all of these are satisfied:

1. Remaining knowledge areas are expanded with the same official-source provenance standard.
2. Full source-body snapshots are retrieved and hashed; V2 review-source hashes are currently canonical URL + title fingerprints only.
3. Dynamic sources have scheduled change monitoring and impact analysis.
4. An authorised human reviewer certifies coverage for the exact immutable pack version.
5. The lawful/copyright boundary remains intact: no copied or translated Guide for New Residents text is introduced.

## Interpretation

`39 concepts / 117 questions` means substantially broader **verified public-source coverage**. It is not a claim of 100% Life in the UK Test syllabus coverage because GOV.UK states that the test is based on the official Guide for New Residents, whose full copyrighted contents are not being reproduced into CitizenAI.
